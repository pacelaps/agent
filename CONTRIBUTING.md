# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in the PaceLaps Agent.

- **Bug reports and ideas** are welcome at support@pacelaps.com. Please include the agent's version (About in the
  tray menu) and, if you can, the newest `agent-*.log` from the log folder (the menu's "Open the log folder"). The
  logs contain no passwords or tokens.
- **Code contributions:** for now we don't accept pull requests. The agent is developed together with Race Control,
  and we want to keep the code's licensing in one hand while the product takes shape. If you've found a fix, tell us
  about it by email and we'll take it from there.

You're free to use, study, change and share the agent under the terms of the GPL-3.0 ([LICENSE](LICENSE)).
